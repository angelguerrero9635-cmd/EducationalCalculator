## Order and coverage, in short

- **University Physics I.** OpenStax and Taylor follow kinematics → forces → energy → momentum →
  rotation → oscillations; Simple Nature puts conservation of energy first (chapter 2) and forces
  after momentum. Gravitation and fluids are chapters in every survey book (OpenStax V1 13–14, GRE
  items) with no taxonomy topic; units and estimation open every book (no topic).
- **University Physics II.** Same order everywhere: charge and field → Gauss → potential →
  capacitance → current and circuits → magnetism → induction → AC. Heat and kinetic theory open
  OpenStax V2 (chapters 1–4) but belong to no University Physics topic; they are filed under
  thermal-statistical #0.
- **University Physics III.** Geometric optics (OpenStax V3 2, GRE items), atomic structure,
  condensed matter and particle physics are chapters with no taxonomy topic. Electromagnetic waves
  close OpenStax V2 and open the waves topic here.
- **Classical Mechanics.** Taylor, 8.09, 8.223 and Cline all add noninertial frames, chaos and
  scattering (gaps); Taylor teaches Lagrange (7) before central forces (8) and Hamilton late (13).
- **Electromagnetism.** Griffiths and 8.07 open with vector analysis and close with radiation and
  relativity (radiation filed under EM waves; relativistic electrodynamics a gap); fields in matter
  are their own chapters in Griffiths (4, 6). Ellingson adds transmission lines, waveguides and
  antennas (engineering, no physics topic).
- **Quantum.** Griffiths and 8.05 add identical particles, tensor products, the variational method,
  WKB and scattering (gaps); 8.05 teaches the linear-algebra formalism before angular momentum.
- **Thermal and statistical.** 8.044 opens with four probability lectures (no physics topic);
  Schroeder teaches engines (4) after the statistical second law (2–3); interacting systems and the
  Ising model are a gap.
