# Electrical: topics against open textbooks and courses

Reference only (see `../../README.md`): nothing here goes into a lesson, picture or test. Generated by the R4 crosswalk script from `../toc/college/*.json` and `../../questions/college/*.jsonl`; for each topic, the unit of every source that teaches it, in the source’s own order, with the worked-example kinds, number ranges and notation recorded in the table of contents (our words).

**What the sources show (2026-10-02).** Open electrical sources reached: Kuphaldt's _Modular Electronics Learning_ (CC BY 4.0, questions copied), MIT OpenCourseWare 6.003, 6.061, 6.02, 6.013 and 16.06 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, types), and two Wikibooks _Control Systems_ pages (CC BY-SA 4.0). **Order and coverage.** ModEL teaches series, parallel, KVL/KCL, series-parallel, network theorems, then capacitors and inductors with time constants, before AC (phasors, reactance, impedance, power factor, filters, resonance, transformers, polyphase): the circuits-1 → circuits-2 split of the taxonomy. Its questions are mostly multi-step "complete the table" and design-a-value items with SPICE checks; diagnostic (troubleshooting) questions have no taxonomy home. 6.003 orders signals as time-domain systems → Z and Laplace → convolution → frequency response and feedback → Fourier → sampling, so Laplace comes before Fourier (the taxonomy lists Fourier first). 6.061 mixes power systems with electromechanics (magnetic circuits, DC and induction machines) and symmetrical components. 6.02 combines information theory, channel coding, LTI channels and MAC protocols; multiple access has no topic. 6.013 spends its second half on transmission lines and antennas. Most 16.06 homework is textbook (Franklin, Powell, Emami-Naeini) and was skipped.

Sources: `kuphaldt-model`, `mit-ocw-16-06`, `mit-ocw-6-003`, `mit-ocw-6-013`, `mit-ocw-6-02`, `mit-ocw-6-061`, `wikibooks-control-systems`.
