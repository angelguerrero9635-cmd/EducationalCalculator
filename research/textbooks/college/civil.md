# Civil: topics against open textbooks and courses

Reference only (see `../../README.md`): nothing here goes into a lesson, picture or test. Generated by the R4 crosswalk script from `../toc/college/*.json` and `../../questions/college/*.jsonl`; for each topic, the unit of every source that teaches it, in the source’s own order, with the worked-example kinds, number ranges and notation recorded in the table of contents (our words).

**What the sources show (2026-10-02).** Open civil sources reached: MIT OpenCourseWare (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: 1.060, 1.85, 1.061, 1.34, 1.051, 1.054, 1.364, 1.571), Wikibooks _Fundamentals of Transportation_ (CC BY-SA 4.0, questions copied with attribution), Udoeyo's _Structural Analysis_ (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), Verruijt's _Soil Mechanics_ (free, no licence: titles and types only) and FHWA HEC-22 (public domain, worked examples copied). **Order and coverage.** Udoeyo teaches loads and load combinations, determinate beams, frames and trusses, arches and cables, then two chapters of deflections (double integration, virtual work) before influence lines and the force, slope-deflection and moment-distribution methods: the deflection chapters have no taxonomy topic, as the plan says. Verruijt (and 1.364) spend whole chapters on permeability, flow nets, lateral earth pressure, sheet piles, slopes and piles, all outside the five soil topics. 1.060 teaches pipe systems with pumps and system curves and open channels with gradually varied profiles (M1, S1, …), which the plan lacks. Wikibooks covers traffic flow, shock waves, queueing (M/M/1, D/D/1), signals (HCM delay, cycle length), sight distance, grades and vehicle resistances, horizontal and vertical curves, but has no pavement or freeway-capacity pages. HEC-22 adds gutter spread, inlet interception, orifice and weir outlets and reservoir routing to stormwater design. No open source reached teaches surveying, pavement design, compaction or steel beam and connection design with problems; those topics have few or no records.

Sources: `dma-geodesy-for-the-layman`, `fhwa-hec-22`, `mit-ocw-1-051`, `mit-ocw-1-054`, `mit-ocw-1-060`, `mit-ocw-1-061`, `mit-ocw-1-34`, `mit-ocw-1-364`, `mit-ocw-1-571`, `mit-ocw-1-85`, `udoeyo-structural-analysis`, `verruijt-soil-mechanics`, `wikibooks-fundamentals-of-transportation`.
