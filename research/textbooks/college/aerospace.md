# Aerospace: topics against open textbooks and courses

Reference only (see `../../README.md`): nothing here goes into a lesson, picture or test. Generated by the R4 crosswalk script from `../toc/college/*.json` and `../../questions/college/*.jsonl`; for each topic, the unit of every source that teaches it, in the source’s own order, with the worked-example kinds, number ranges and notation recorded in the table of contents (our words).

**What the sources show (2026-10-02).** The open aerospace sources reached are MIT OpenCourseWare courses (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: 16.01–16.04 Unified fluids, 16.001, 16.07, 16.100, 16.120, 16.20, 16.333, 16.346, 16.50, 16.512), NASA Glenn's _Beginner's Guide to Aeronautics_ and JPL's _Basics of Space Flight_ (titles). Leishman's _Introduction to Aerospace Flight Vehicles_ (eaglepubs.erau.edu) returned HTTP 403 to this environment and was skipped; Bar-Meir's Potto site did not resolve. Order: every course teaches airfoils (thin-airfoil theory) before finite wings (lifting line) and both before compressibility; Unified teaches compressible flow as stagnation quantities → normal shocks → pitot → oblique shocks and expansions → nozzles, the plan's order. Flight mechanics courses start from performance and static stability and spend most time on dynamic modes and autopilots (state space), which the plan treats in one topic each. Structures courses (16.20, 16.001) open with elasticity, composites' engineering constants and thermal stress, then torsion of open and closed sections, shell beams and buckling, and end with structural dynamics; fatigue appears only as limit and ultimate loads and margins of safety. Propulsion (16.50) spends half the term on rockets (staging, nozzles, cooling, thermochemistry) and half on air-breathing engines (cycle, matching, turbomachinery). Orbital mechanics (16.07, 16.346) moves from the two-body problem and Kepler's equation to Lambert's problem, patched conics and relative motion.

Sources: `jpl-basics-of-space-flight`, `mit-ocw-16-001`, `mit-ocw-16-01`, `mit-ocw-16-07`, `mit-ocw-16-100`, `mit-ocw-16-120`, `mit-ocw-16-20`, `mit-ocw-16-333`, `mit-ocw-16-346`, `mit-ocw-16-50`, `mit-ocw-16-512`, `nasa-glenn-beginners-guide`.
