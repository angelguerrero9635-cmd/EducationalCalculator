**2.2. Structure & Properties of Matter**

- _One. Lesson Set One (Lessons 1-10)_
  - 1. How do we play with toys?

  - 4. How can we change objects and materials so we can better use them to build our toys?
  - 5. What happens to materials when they are heated or cooled?

  - 8. How can we use our design sketches to build a toy?
  - 9. How can we finalize our toy designs?
  - 10. How can we share our toy designs with the kindergartners?

**2.3. Habitats & Biodiversity**

- _One. Lesson Set One (Lessons 1-5)_
  - 1. Where can we observe plants and animals?
  - 2. What is the land like in places where plants and animals live?
  - 3. Where is water found, and what is it like?
  - 4. How can we show the location, shapes, and kinds of land and water in a place?
  - 5. How are places where plants and animals live similar and different?
- _Two. Lesson Set Two (Lessons 6-10)_

  - 7. What kinds of plants and animals live in each national park?
  - 8. What kinds of plants and animals live on land and in water?

  - 10. What kinds of plants and animals live in different places?

  - 6. How can seeds in fruits get moved to new places?

  - 8. How are our plants in different conditions similar and/or different?
  - 9. Did plants need light and water to keep growing and stay healthy? How do we know?
  - 10. How do plants keep growing in different places?
