## robots.txt as read

- openstax.org: `User-agent: *` disallows `/accounts`, `/admin`, `/l/`, `/r/`, `/confirmation/`, `/general`, `/contents`, `/extras`, `/errata`, `/resources`, `/apps/archive`, `/apps/archive-preview`, `/apps/cms/api/spike`; `/books/` disallowed for GPTBot. Fetched: `/rex/sitemaps/*.xml`, `/apps/cms/api/books/`, `/apps/cms/api/v2/pages/<id>/`, the `/details/` pages (client-rendered; titles came from the sitemaps).
- activecalculus.org: `Crawl-Delay: 20` (kept). opentext.uleth.ca: none (404). www.apexcalculus.com: AI crawlers share the `*` group, which disallows only shop and search paths.
- personal.math.ubc.ca, understandinglinearalgebra.org, linear.pugetsound.edu, jheffero.w3.uvm.edu: none (404). www.jirka.org: disallows `/cgi-bin`. hefferon.net: disallows `/wp-admin/`.
- math.libretexts.org: `Crawl-delay: 5`, `Request-rate: 1/5`, disallows `/Special:*`, `/Template:*`, `/User:*`, `/deki/`, `/*action=*`, `/@*` (kept).
- ocw.mit.edu: `Allow: /`. math.mit.edu: `Crawl-delay: 5` (kept). apcentral.collegeboard.org: disallows admin, search, user paths only. www.ets.org: no relevant disallow.
- cengage.com, pearson.com, wiley.com, media.wiley.com: product and catalog pages allowed. open.umn.edu asks `Crawl-delay: 120`; not used.

## What was recorded, skipped, and why

- **Text records** come only from CC BY / CC BY-SA books: Active Calculus (162, all static section exercises; WeBWorK library exercises skipped because the Open Problem Library's licence is not the book's), Active Calculus – Multivariable (76), Lebl (66, under the CC BY-SA option, answers cut), Austin (78).
- **Type records**: MIT OCW exams (18.01SC exams 1–4 and final, 18.02SC exams 1–3 and final, 18.03SC exams 1–3 and final, 18.06SC exams 1–2, 18.06 Spring 2010 exams 1–3 and final), AP Calculus AB 2023–2026 (24) and BC-only questions 2023–2026 (12), CLP-2 and CLP-4 problem books (66), GRE Mathematics practice book (27, calculus/ODE/linear algebra items only).
- 18.06SC exam 3 and final PDFs have no text layer (font encoding); replaced by 18.06 Spring 2010 exams. 18.02SC exam 4 was not reached by the page crawl.
- **AP Calculus**: the plan's targets (40 calc-1, 40 calc-2) are not reachable from AP Central, which serves only 2023–2026: 24 AB and 12 BC-only questions recorded; the other gap is filled by OCW and the open books.
- **NCEES FE** sample questions: off limits (brief). The FE specification adds nothing for mathematics beyond the topic list and was not used.
- **OpenStax exercises**: off limits; titles only.
- **Stewart and Boyce–DiPrima**: the cengage.com product page looped on redirects for our client; Stewart chapter titles 1–11 and chapter 12–16 section titles are from Cengage's pages as indexed by a web search. Boyce–DiPrima: Wiley's public TOC excerpt gave chapters 1–9 and sections of chapters 1–2 only.
- **Discrepancies resolved**: Active Calculus licence (2.0 badge on the home page vs 4.0 in the books: 4.0); OCW licence (confirmed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); Lebl (dual; CC BY-SA used); Hefferon (dual; CC BY-SA 3.0 US used); Beezer (GFDL, confirmed); OpenStax Calculus (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, confirmed from the CMS).
