are pictures for the pictures chat (brief: `docs/RENDERINGS_HS_ROUND_2.md`, tracker entries H89
on). Each names the plans (m.9 = Grade 9 math) and pages waiting on it; the builders list the same
pages in `docs/build/<plan>.md`. Status: open, done.
