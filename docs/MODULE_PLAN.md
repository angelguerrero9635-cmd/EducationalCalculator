## Section notes

- **Section 1 (Math K–2).** 28 main modules plus 20 problem-type modules (`<skill>~<slug>`),
  each on its own page. They cover the common test and textbook question types found by the
  reviews:
  - compare word problems (to 20 and to 100); comparing numbers with >, < and =
  - tape-diagram word problems; two-step problems on a number line with hops
  - adding up to four numbers; subtracting tens from tens; 10 or 100 more or less
  - expanded form; trading tens and ones (e.g. 3 hundreds, 14 tens, 5 ones)
  - "how many more" in picture graphs and bar graphs; line plots
  - true or false number sentences on a balance; heavier and lighter on a balance
  - polygons by sides and angles; rows and columns of squares
  - lengths on a number line; money left after buying

  Not modelled (no quantity to calculate): position words (K.G.1), a.m./p.m., and 3D solids
  beyond the assumptions.

  **Follow-up list (proposed by the second review, not built yet):**
  - K: number partners of 5 and 10 (number bond); compare capacity; compose shapes from
    pattern blocks (K.G.6).
  - Grade 1: order three objects by length (1.MD.1); compare-and-combine word problems;
    unequal parts ("is this a half?"); irregular polygons.
  - Grade 2: equal groups (2.OA.4 with circles instead of an array); broken-ruler and
    two-unit measuring (2.MD.2, 2.MD.4); bills as well as coins; picture graph with 4
    categories; a.m./p.m. toggle on the clock.
