## Section notes

- **Section 1 (Math K–2).** 32 skill modules (including the two new Kindergarten skills,
  position words and putting shapes together) plus 32 problem-type modules (`<skill>~<slug>`),
  each on its own page. They cover the common test and textbook question types found by the
  reviews:
  - compare word problems (to 20 and to 100), compare-then-add; comparing numbers with >, < and =
  - number partners (number bonds); tape-diagram word problems; two-step problems with hops
  - adding up to four numbers; subtracting tens from tens; 10 or 100 more or less
  - expanded form; trading tens and ones (e.g. 3 hundreds, 14 tens, 5 ones)
  - "how many more" in picture graphs and bar graphs; a 4-category picture graph; line plots
  - true or false number sentences, heavier and lighter (balance); which jar holds more
  - order three lengths; broken ruler; feet and inches; lengths on a number line
  - equal parts or not; shapes that look different; faces, edges and corners of solids
  - polygons by sides and angles; rows and columns of squares; equal groups
  - bills; money left after buying; a.m./p.m. on the clock
